
## Hint
Steps per segment behave like `S0 * (1 + c/B)`, where `c` moves through the run and jumps when the corpus switches. Two different batch sizes in a window identify `c`; the per-segment time-optimal batch is `sqrt(c * t_overhead * throughput)`, not `c`. Since `c` keeps growing, estimate its trend and aim slightly ahead of where it is now.
