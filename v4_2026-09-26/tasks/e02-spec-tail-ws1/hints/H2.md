## Hint (level H2)

The mechanisms active in this lab are exactly the following (all others are switched off):

- **Roofline: arithmetic intensity and the critical batch for decoding**: `step time = max(bytes_moved/BW, FLOPs/P_peak); the batch at which decoding stops being memory-bound is B_crit = (P_peak/BW)*bytes_per_param`
- **Speculative decoding with positionally decaying acceptance**: `acceptance at draft position i is a_i = a*rho^i within the positions the deployment's own proposal length exercises, and continues as a_i = a*rho^(k-1)*rho_tail^(i-k+1) beyond position k (rho_tail = rho unless the lab states otherwise); expected accepted tokens per step E = sum_{i<=g} prod_{k<i} a_k; improvement factor IF = E/(g*c + 1)`
- **KV-cache capacity sets the feasible batch**: `per-token KV bytes = 2*L*H_kv*d_head*bytes; B_max = floor((M_gpu*mem_util - weights - activations)/(per_token*seq_len))`

The functional forms above are exact; their constants are unknown and must be measured.
