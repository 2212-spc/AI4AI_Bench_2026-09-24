## Hint (level H2)

The mechanisms active in this lab are exactly the following (all others are switched off):

- **Reward-model overoptimization**: `with d = sqrt(KL(policy || init)): gold score R_bon(d) = d*(alpha - beta*d) for best-of-n, R_rl(d) = d*(alpha - beta*log d) for policy gradient; the PROXY reward is monotone in d, so the peak is invisible in the proxy`
- **KL budget as the invariant, not the knob that sets it**: `best-of-n: KL = log n - (n-1)/n exactly; policy gradient: KL(steps) rises and saturates, KL = kl_cap*(1 - exp(-steps/steps0)); a KL penalty acts as early stopping`
- **Length as the dominant reward channel**: `mean length grows with d; the proxy reward is wq*(quality term) + wl*tanh((len-len0)/sl), so a length-only policy can reproduce most of the proxy gain`

The functional forms above are exact; their constants are unknown and must be measured.
